## How To Use This Matrix

### For Consumer (P2P)
Start with **one vertical** where the "borrow vs buy" math is most obvious:
- Camping/outdoor gear
- Home DIY tools
- Camera equipment
- Event furniture
- Baby/kid items

### For B2B
Start with **one industry** where idle asset pain is acute:
- Construction (heavy equipment + tools)
- Events (everything is seasonal and idle between gigs)
- Agriculture (seasonal machinery demand)
- Media production (gear is expensive, jobs are project-based)

### Listing Prioritization Principle
Ask for each item: *"Would someone use this less than 5 times per year and hate storing it?"*
If yes, it belongs on your platform.
